## 1. Introduction and General Principles
This Privacy Policy describes the principles applied by the **GenToLo** application with regard to the protection of personal data, in full compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR"), Italian Legislative Decree 196/2003 (as amended by Legislative Decree 101/2018), the ePrivacy Directive (2002/58/EC) and the Google Play *User Data Policy*.

GenToLo is developed according to the principles of **Privacy by Design and Privacy by Default** (Art. 25 GDPR): it is a purely local utility designed to operate with no collection, tracking, profiling or transmission of personal data whatsoever.

---

## 2. No Collection or Processing of Personal Data
The application **does not carry out any processing of personal data** within the meaning of Art. 4(2) GDPR:
* **No identifying or personal details**: The application does not require an account or registration, and does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers or other personal details.
* **No device identifiers**: IP addresses, IMEI codes, Android ID, Advertising ID (AAID) and other unique hardware identifiers are neither acquired nor stored.
* **No geolocation data**: The application does not access GPS or network location services.
* **No financial data**: The application does not handle real-money transactions and does not request or store credit card or bank account details.

All algorithmic calculations and the random generation of number combinations take place ephemerally in the device's volatile memory (RAM), with no persistent storage of the numbers drawn. The application stores on the user's device only the technical interface configuration preferences (the quantity of numbers to draw and the composition of the board), containing no personal data and with no transmission to external servers or databases.

---

## 3. Offline Operation and System Permissions
* **100% Offline Operation**: The release version of GenToLo does not request permission to access the internet (the `android.permission.INTERNET` permission is absent from the application manifest). This makes it technically impossible for any information to be transmitted, communicated or sent from the device to the developer's servers or to third parties.
* **Zero sensitive permissions**: The application does not request permissions to access the contacts list, the media gallery, external storage, the microphone or the camera.
* **System sharing (ACTION_SEND)**: The optional function for sharing the generated combination uses only the standard system Intent (*Android Share Sheet* / *iOS Activity View*). The data transferred consists solely of the text string of the generated numbers. GenToLo does not store, track or receive any information about the third-party application chosen by the user (e.g. a messaging client or clipboard) or about the recipients. The selected third-party application acts as an independent Data Controller; any joint controllership under Art. 26 GDPR, or liability of the developer for the processing carried out by such application, is excluded.

---

## 4. No Cookies, Tracking or Third-Party SDKs
* **Zero cookies and tracking storage**: The application does not use cookies, advertising identifiers, fingerprinting or similar tracking technologies.
* **No advertising or analytics SDKs**: GenToLo does not integrate advertising libraries (e.g. Google AdMob), analytics frameworks (e.g. Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics), or telemetry and crash-reporting tools that transmit data externally.

## 5. Minors and Purpose of the Software
* **Minors' data**: As the application does not collect any personal data, it does not process data relating to minors (Art. 8 GDPR and Art. 2-quinquies of Legislative Decree 196/2003).
* **Purpose of the software**: GenToLo is a number generator and a mathematical/algorithmic simulator for recreational purposes. The application does not handle bets, does not involve monetary transactions or cash winnings, and has no connection with gambling or prize competitions.

## 6. Data Subject Rights and Support
* **Exercise of rights under Arts. 15–22 GDPR**: Since the developer retains no personal data or identifiers that would allow the user to be identified (Art. 11 GDPR), it is not practically possible to act on requests for access, rectification, erasure or portability relating to the use of the local application.
* **Support requests via email**: Should the user voluntarily contact the developer at the email address provided for technical assistance, the data communicated (sender's address and message text) will be used exclusively to respond to the request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and will not be disclosed to third parties.
* **Complaint to the Supervisory Authority**: The user's right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (*Garante per la Protezione dei Dati Personali*, Piazza Venezia 11, 00187 Rome, Italy — www.garanteprivacy.it) remains unaffected.
